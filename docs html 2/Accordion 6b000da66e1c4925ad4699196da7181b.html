Accordion

Figma linkStorybook link

The accordion component provides large amounts of content in a small space by hiding it behind panels. The title gives an overview of the content, allowing users to decide at a glance whether to reveal the panel content or not.

When applied to content-rich pages, accordions can minimise scrolling and make the page appear less daunting to read. Remember that users may not read all content behind an accordion, so avoid hiding critical information inside a panel.

Use when

  • Reducing the amount of content on a page.
  • Organising related content into groups.
  • Sharing additional or optional content.

Don't use when

  • Sharing content that's essential for users to complete their journey.

Elements

  1. Panel – Includes the title of the content section and is the touch target.
  1. Icon – Chevron icon that indicates if the panel is collapsed or expanded.
  1. Dropdown – Expanded content from the panel.
  1. Badge (optional) – Used to communicate granular detail about other elements and only applies to the small variant.

Visual

Alignment

Align the chevron icon to the right of the title.

Layout

Always make accordions full width and stack vertically.

Groups

Always group at least two accordion panels together at a time. Although there's no limit on how many can be grouped together on one page.

Separate panels with a Divider at the top and bottom of the group, and between every panel. Don't insert space between panels vertically.

Variants

There are two accordion variants we use, medium and small. The medium is the default and is used in most places. The small is used for tight spaces within cards.

Behaviour

States

Accordions have two states:

  • collapsed with the content hidden behind the heading
  • expanded is when users select the heading and panel content is revealed

The chevron icon indicates which state each individual panel is in – down when collapsed and up when expanded.

Interaction

The touch target to switch between states is the entire heading, not just the icon.

Content

Panel label

  • Each title must be unique from all other titles.
  • Use a concise label that clearly describes the hidden content in the panel. This helps users navigate content without having to expand all panels.

Dropdown description

  • Never hide content that users need to complete their journey behind the dropdown. Insert secondary information only.
  • Use simple content such as text and links.
  • Use form controls only to change how we display data. Never use form controls to collect data such as personal details.
  • Split up longer content with subheadings if appropriate.
  • The first panel can be open by default.
