Table component is used to organise and display data efficiently.

Elements

  1. Title/caption – Title of the table. This is also known as captions.
  1. Header – Title of the column or row.
  1. Value – A short piece of information.
  1. Footer (optional) - Footer of the column.

Visual

Variants

There are two variants of the table, and both have the option of showing striped rows:

Horizontal headersHeaders are positioned at the top of each column.
Vertical headersHeaders are positioned at the start of each row.

Behaviour

  • Should have a maximum of four columns.
  • Should be full width.

Accessibility

Data tables should have a programmatically associated <caption> or name (e.g. via aria-label or aria-labelledby).

This should be documented by design team and supplied to the engineering team on handover.
