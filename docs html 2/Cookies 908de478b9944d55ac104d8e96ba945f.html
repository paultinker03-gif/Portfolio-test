Figma link

Cookies are used to make a website work and collect information about how users may use our service. It uses the Drawer component.

Position

Cookies are always positioned at the bottom of the screen for all screen sizes.

Desktop

The content is always left aligned with buttons side by side.

Tablet

The content is always left aligned with buttons side by side.

Mobile

The content is always left aligned with buttons stacked vertically.

Elements

  1. Heading 5
  1. Body text, including text links to the cookie policy and privacy policy
  1. Secondary button
  1. Primary button

Accepting and rejecting cookies

When a user accepts cookies, all of the website functionality is enabled. Currently when a user rejects the cookies, only the intercom chat feature will be disabled.
