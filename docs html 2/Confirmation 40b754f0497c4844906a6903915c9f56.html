Confirmation

Figma linkStorbook link

Confirmations are whole page moments to communicate a clear message. For example at the end of a multi-step journey, when an error occurs or when page cannot be found.

Use when

  • finishing a multi-step journey
  • loading an action
  • confirming an action was successful
  • communicating that an action was unsuccessful

Variants

There are two variants of a confirmation, depending on the previous or required action:

VariantPurpose
SuccessLets the user know they've successfully completed a task.
ErrorAn error has stopped a user from completing a task, or a page cannot be found.

Elements

  1. Icon: icon changes depending on the variant.
  1. Title: indicates the status of the action which depends on the variant.
  1. Body text (optional): can provide more context and detail around the action and next steps.
  1. Button: provides a next step.

Visual

Alignment

All elements should be centre aligned on the page.

Icons

We use two types of icons at the top of the component, depending on the variant.

Content

Pattern

Success

  • You've done the action eg You've ceased membership, You've saved your changes
  • We've done the action eg We've updated your investment strategy, We've received your payment request

Error

  • Apologise, we couldn't do the action eg Sorry, we couldn't create your letters, Sorry, we couldn't process your payment request

Loading

  • We're doing the action eg We're processing your payment request, We're creating your letters]
